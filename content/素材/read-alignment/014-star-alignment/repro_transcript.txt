  (input 2999, uniq% 99.87, avg len 199.73, mismatch 1.93%).
MAPQ DISTRIBUTION via samtools (REAL, primary alignments):
  run_default :  5990 reads @ MAPQ 255  ;  8 reads @ MAPQ 3   (unique=255)

=> Confirms STAR assigns MAPQ 255 to unique reads (GATK drops it as "unavailable");
